My School/College

About the School

Southville International School and Colleges (SISC) is where I am currently studying as a BSIT student majoring in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. Southville International School and Colleges (SISC) is a private, globally oriented educational institution located in BF Homes International, Las Piñas City, Philippines.

More Details

  • Overview & History
    • Founded: Established in 1990 by Dr. Genevieve Ledesma-Malaro, starting initially as a small school for the intellectually gifted.
    • Location: 1281 Tropical Avenue Corner Luxembourg Street, BF Homes International, Las Piñas City.
    • Offerings: Comprehensive programs ranging from preschool, K-12, undergraduate college degrees, to graduate studies.
  • Accreditations & Recognition
    • School of International Character: Recognized officially by Philippine law under Republic Act 9493 in 2006.
    • International Standards: Accredited by the Western Association of Schools and Colleges (WASC) and certified under ISO standards.
    • Higher Education: Sanctioned with Autonomous Status by the Commission on Higher Education (CHED).

Why I Chose This School

  1. Strong AI/ML curriculum that matches my career goals.
  2. Access to modern facilities and knowledgeable faculty who guide students through real-world AI and software development projects.
  3. A campus environment that encourages collaboration and hands-on learning, which helped me grow both technically and personally.
